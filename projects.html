EDTECH · AI · PRODUCT

Arthic

ARTHIC / PRODUCT IDENTITY

A bilingual English/Urdu exam-preparation platform for Pakistani entrance tests. It brings together an AI mentor, past-paper library, personalised study plans and an exam calendar.

The problem

Preparation is often scattered across guidebooks, coaching centres and unstructured past-paper PDFs. Arthic brings the workflow into one place.

My approach

Built a hand-written TypeScript/Vite frontend, Vercel serverless functions and PostgreSQL-backed services. The product is in early access; the mentor is still being developed, so the site distinguishes live functionality from planned features.

Built with TypeScript/Vite, Vercel serverless functions and PostgreSQL.

RESEARCH · COMPUTER VISION

Diabetic Retinopathy Detection

RESEARCH CONTEXT / ICDR GRADING

A mini-project comparing three AI models on retinal images to explore how they identify signs associated with diabetic retinopathy.

Scope & limitation

The repository labels its checked-in pilot results as synthetic data and seeded mock predictions, not real model inference. This is an exploratory comparison, not a clinical tool or validated diagnostic system.
