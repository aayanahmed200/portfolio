01 / About

Building at the edge of ideas and impact.

A student, researcher and builder interested in how technology can make knowledge more accessible and solve meaningful problems.

I’m Aayan Ahmed, an A-Level student at Aitchison College in Lahore, studying History, Computer Science, Mathematics and Physics. My interests sit across artificial intelligence, education, scientific research, public policy and entrepreneurship.

I’ve worked on AI literacy research at Lahore University of Management Sciences, explored brain-computer interfaces through the New York Academy of Sciences’ Junior Academy, and helped develop a cybersecurity awareness course for Pakistan’s National Cyber Emergency Response Team that was adopted for nationwide implementation by the Ministry of IT & Telecommunications.

Outside research and technology, I care about education access, student leadership, editorial work and community initiatives. I founded Nishaan Education and co-founded Pak Vision, working with volunteers and partners to create practical opportunities for others.
